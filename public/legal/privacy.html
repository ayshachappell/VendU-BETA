Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

VendU is operated by Integro Service Group LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • School email address — used only to verify you are a student and to sign you in.
  • Profile and storefront information you choose to enter (name, photo, bio, services, prices, payment handles, social links, graduation year, campus).
  • Activity — posts, messages, bookings, reviews and referrals you create in the app.
  • Phone number — only if you provide one for appointment text confirmations and reminders.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share your email with vendors or other students. We do not process payments. VendU is a peer-to-peer technology platform: payments happen directly between you and the other student through their own third-party payment app (Cash App, Venmo, Zelle, PayPal, etc.). We never hold, escrow, route or refund funds, we do not receive your payment-app credentials, and we assume no liability for transfers, misdirected payments, or offline disputes, service quality or incidents between users. Always double-check a handle before sending money.

How your data is used

To verify student status, show you campus-specific listings, deliver messages and booking notifications, and prevent abuse.

Reports and moderation

When you report a profile or listing, we store the report (what was reported, the reason, your email and the time) so our trust & safety team at support@venduapp.com can review it and, where needed, deactivate or remove content. Reports are visible only to VendU administrators and to you.

AI auto-fill photos

When you tap “Auto-fill with AI”, the photo you selected is sent to our AI provider to generate a suggested title, category, description and price. The photo is used only for that suggestion, is not sold, and is not used to identify you.

Staying signed in

Your sign-in is stored on your device so you only verify once. It stays there until you tap Log out or delete your account.

Our content and yours

The app’s code, design, logos and likeness belong to Integro Service Group LLC and may not be copied — see the Terms of Service. Content you upload stays yours; you are responsible for having the rights to it.

Retention & deletion

Accounts inactive for one year, and accounts that can no longer be verified after their graduation year, are automatically deleted. You may delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete my account, or at this page. Deletion is permanent.

Children

VendU is for enrolled college students and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Questions or requests: info@integroservicegroup.com
