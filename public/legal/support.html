Common questions

I never got my verification email. Check spam and your school's quarantine. School mail systems sometimes delay outside mail by a few minutes. Then request the link again.

The link opened but the app is still locked. Keep the app tab open — it checks every few seconds and unlocks automatically. If you opened the link on a different device, just re-enter the same email on this device.

My school isn't listed. Any .edu address works. Search your campus in the campus picker; email us if it's missing.

How do I get paid as a vendor? Add your payment handles (Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, Zelle, Apple Pay, Google Pay) under Profile → Set up your storefront. Students tap them to pay you directly. VendU never touches the money.

How do I become a founder? Refer 3 vendors with your referral link. Each campus has 10 founder spots.

How do I delete my account? Profile → Delete my account, or use this page.

Report a safety issue

Email info@integroservicegroup.com with "SAFETY" in the subject and we will review immediately.
