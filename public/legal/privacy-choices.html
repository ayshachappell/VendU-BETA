State privacy rights

If you live in California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana, Delaware, Iowa, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Minnesota, Maryland, Tennessee, Indiana, Kentucky, Rhode Island or any other state with a comprehensive privacy law, you may request access to, correction of, a copy of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling. To make a request, email info@integroservicegroup.com from your account email, or delete your account at any time at this page. We respond within 45 days and may extend once when allowed by law. You may appeal a denied request by replying to our decision; authorized agents may submit requests with written permission.

Nevada residents may submit a verified request not to sell covered information to the same address. We do not knowingly sell the personal information of anyone under 16.
